# 100 x 60 x 50 mm hole-box 3D test

Tested the corrected open-top shell model with 2 mm nominal wall, a centered
20 mm bottom through-hole, two 4 mm gates, and two 2 mm screening vents.

- CAD volume: `41323.68146928206 mm3`; analytic mismatch `5.3e-16`.
- SI mesh bounds: `(0,0,0)` to `(0.1,0.06,0.05)` m.
- Standard OpenFOAM `checkMesh -allTopology`: `Mesh OK`.
- Gate/vent patch audit: `PASS`; centroids at requested positions.
- Isothermal solver: two runs reached `0.003 s` and `End`; final U hashes
  identical.
- Virtual thermal/Tait solver: reached `0.0001 s` and `End`; max Co below
  `2e-4`, but temperature-bound events remain, so thermal/warpage output is
  still screening-only.
- Python regression tests: `10 passed`.

The strict `checkMesh -allGeometry` determinant warning for thin tetrahedral
cells remains a follow-up quality item even though the standard topology gate
passes.
