# INC-SUS-SURFACE-20261108-01 — stainless surface defect investigation controls

Observed 2026-11-08 JST. Sanitized methodological knowledge only; no customer, product identity, dimensions, private correspondence or production yields are included.

Rust-colored deposits on stainless steel are not by themselves proof of substrate corrosion. Distinguish transferred iron/oxide, abrasive or metal smut, organic/inorganic wash residues, genuine substrate attack, and roughness-driven optical differences. Elemental Fe/O alone is insufficient: use controls, spatial chemistry, phase/chemical-state identification and before/after morphology as applicable.

Verify powder mass versus solution volume/concentration, machine frequency versus actual rotation speed, process-stage definitions, wash-bath condition and provenance before changing settings. A lower aggregate defect rate can coexist with worse results within some production strata. Preserve batch, process-order and rewash histories; compare split populations and independent process replicates.

DEM can study media/workpiece contact and stacking, local FEM mechanical damage, and CFD rinsing/drainage. These do not identify rust chemistry. Calibrate against the actual machine type and measurements. Before dispatch, require native checkpoint/resume proof, input hashes, resource admission and bounded supervision.
