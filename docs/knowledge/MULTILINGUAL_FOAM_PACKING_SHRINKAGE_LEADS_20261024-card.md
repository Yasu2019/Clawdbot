# Multilingual injection-molding research leads ? 2026-10-24

## Verified primary evidence

- Japanese J-STAGE paper (2021), DOI 10.4325/seikeikakou.33.213, measured thermally expandable microcapsule foaming across four PP homopolymers and two impact copolymers. It reports capillary viscosity at 260 C, 170 x 10 x 4 mm specimens, 1.4 wt% capsules, 260 C melt, 50 C mold, mold-back and short-shot routes, density and SEM cell-size measurements. Lower shear viscosity tracked greater expansion/larger cells; the cell-size profile differed between mold-back and short-shot routes. This supports a distinct expandable-capsule mechanism. It does not validate conventional air traps, dissolved-gas voids, or ordinary short-shot defects. [Article](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/seikeikakou/33/6/33_213/_article/-char/ja) [PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/seikeikakou/33/6/33_213/_pdf/-char/ja)
- German JKU institutional record for Wuschko (2016) describes OpenFOAM packing/cooling solver experiments with two geometries, two materials and five volume-flow conditions. The record does not identify grades or expose raw series; treat as a follow-up lead, not a PP benchmark. [JKU record](https://research.jku.at/de/publications/numerische-und-experimentelle-untersuchungen-der-nachdruck-und-ab/)
- Spanish CIATEC thesis search record suggests PP homopolymer dimensional-shrinkage experiments across injection/holding-pressure/holding-time factors; direct PDF retrieval returned 403, so material/geometry/results/license are unverified. [Repository PDF](https://ciatec.repositorioinstitucional.mx/jspui/bitstream/1019/47/1/Tesis%20Final_Jaime%20Navarrete.pdf)

## Use gate

Do not transfer these results as generic PP coefficients. Retrieve and identify the German thesis materials and raw observables; resolve access and inspect the Spanish thesis; digitize Japanese plotted/table values with source, units and uncertainty. Do not mix microcapsule foaming with trapped air or dissolved-gas nucleation. No solver code or material coefficients were changed from this evidence.
