AI VIDEO CONTROL PROTOCOL - HONKI EDITION

GOAL
Turn AI video generation from probabilistic "slot machine production" into human-directed, repeatable production.

CORE PRINCIPLE
The human remains the director.
The AI is a tool, not the author.

FOUR CONTROL LAYERS
1. Structured intent:
   Use JSON instead of vague natural language prompts.
2. Pose constraints:
   Fix start and end frames with human-defined composition.
3. Human motion:
   Feed real motion reference to recover body realism.
4. Differential revision:
   Revise only the failing block, not the whole prompt.

WHY AI LOOKS "AI-GENERATED"
- weak temporal consistency
- unstable identity
- synthetic body timing
- camera drift
- random composition reinterpretation
- uncontrolled interpolation

BEST PRACTICE
- separate camera / character / motion / lighting / restrictions
- define exclusions explicitly
- review with a scoring sheet
- version the JSON
- reuse proven templates

MINIMUM LOOP
1. define objective
2. build JSON
3. prepare start/end pose
4. record motion reference
5. generate short shot
6. review
7. patch JSON
8. regenerate

DO NOT
- edit too many variables at once
- trust vague cinematic prompts alone
- skip review notes
- jump directly to long scenes
