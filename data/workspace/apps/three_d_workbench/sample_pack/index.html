3D Workbench Sample Pack

This sample shows the recommended folder-first workflow for 3D work. It is intentionally light: no engines, no background jobs, just a reusable structure and a small manifest.

ExportReady

E:\Assets\3D\ExportReady\SamplePack
Cache
Keep cache separate from source files.
E:\Assets\3D\Cache\SamplePack
