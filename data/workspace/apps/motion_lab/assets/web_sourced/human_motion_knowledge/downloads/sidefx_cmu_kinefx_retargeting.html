RETARGET THE CMU MOTION CAPTURE DATABASE

made in Houdini 19.5 for Intermediate by Luca Pataracchia at SideFX

5/5

(2 responses)

Category Animation,  Character,  Crowds,  Rigging
PostedApril 04, 2023

TOTAL DURATION: 1h 1m 59s

Learn how to use Houdini’s KineFX character tools and a TOP network to retarget the Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database to match a rig of our choosing. This lesson includes a workflow for procedurally removing noise from the mocap data and a description on how to turn your KineFX rig into an agent for crowd simulations. This workflow provides a template for converting old crowd animations onto a new skeleton.
